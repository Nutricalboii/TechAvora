# Avora Ventures backlink strategy

This plan supports the canonical site at https://www.avoraventure.com/ and avoids paid, automated, or low-quality link schemes.

## Priority link targets

1. The home page: brand and broad production-AI positioning.
2. `/services`: synthetic data, annotation, labeling, quality assurance, and AI implementation service pages.
3. `/work`: anonymized delivery outcomes and methodology evidence.
4. `/blog`: original explainers and research references.

## Best-fit sources

- Partner, client, and implementation ecosystem pages where Avora has a genuine relationship.
- Data, AI, MLOps, and enterprise engineering communities that accept original technical contributions.
- University, accelerator, incubator, and conference speaker or mentor profiles when earned.
- Vendor and tooling directories that manually review companies and clearly disclose listings.
- Case-study, podcast, webinar, and interview pages that cite a specific Avora contribution.

## Content assets to earn links

- A practical guide comparing synthetic data validation methods.
- A data-annotation quality checklist with agreement metrics and acceptance gates.
- An anonymized AI data lifecycle case study with reproducible methodology.
- A quarterly original research note using clearly attributed data and transparent limitations.

## Anchor and destination rules

- Prefer branded anchors such as “Avora Ventures” and naked URLs.
- Use descriptive anchors sparingly and only when they accurately describe the destination.
- Keep links distributed across the home, services, work, and genuinely relevant research pages.
- Never exchange links solely for ranking, publish spun articles, or buy bulk links.

## Measurement

Track referring domains, referral sessions, engaged sessions, assisted conversions, and qualified inquiries in Search Console and GA4. Review new links monthly, reject suspicious placements, and record the source, target URL, anchor, relationship, and first-seen date in the marketing log.
